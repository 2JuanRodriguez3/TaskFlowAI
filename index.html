About Me

Hi, I'm Juan Rodriguez, an Advanced Computer Science student at UAT with a passion for AI, software development, and LEGO building.

Problem

LEGO enthusiasts often have large collections of unused or leftover pieces. Existing tools focus on official sets and instructions, not creative reuse of inventory.

Innovation / Solution

BrickBrain combines artificial intelligence and inventory management, with LEGO creativity to help users discover new and unique build possibilities from their existing collections.

Design Process

Research

Studied LEGO Builder and BrickLink Studio to identify gaps in creative generation tools.

Planning

Designed inventory system and AI recommendation structure.

Development

Built responsive web prototype using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Testing

Improved suggestion logic and UI experience for usability.
